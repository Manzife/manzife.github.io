Pricing & Experimentation Lab

Interactive tools for experiment design and causal inference.

Pricing is an inference problem: every price is a hypothesis about customer behavior, and every experiment is a way to test it. This lab collects small interactive tools I use to reason about experiment design — the kind of quick sanity-checks a Growth or Pricing team runs before, during, and after a test.

Sample Size Calculator

Before running an A/B test, you need to know how many users you need to reliably detect the effect you care about. Too small a sample and you'll miss real effects or chase noise; too large and you waste time and traffic. This calculator tells you the minimum sample size for a given baseline rate, effect size, and confidence requirements.
